# NOCTIVORE FX v10.9 — Creature Forest

## Creative direction
Original dark-forest psychedelic FX instrument informed by the broad DarkPsy/forest/psycore sound-design vocabulary. No third-party samples, algorithms, artworks, presets or product identity reused.

## Sound
Three new automated macro controls: Predator Throat, Spore Code and Burrow Interference. Continuous sample-linked organism modulation; raised-cosine event envelopes. Twelve contrasting factory scenes use these with more purposeful FM, spectral folding, and slow autonomous A/B morphing. Previous v10.8 click smoothing and 8–24-voice polyphony settings remain.

## Interface
- Atlas tabs corrected to printed artwork coordinates (formerly 21 px too high)
- Fixed 1491:1055 aspect ratio on resize, smaller 1044 × 739 default laptop window
- Original tree/mushroom art cropped into all four independent opaque subpages without printed old knobs/labels
- Reduced cross-page control collisions via grid gutters; stronger themed combo styling
- Atlas's printed painted keyboard remains clickable without ghosting a second generic keyboard
- New forest organism controls on Morphology, with Predator Talk, Spore Disruption, and Burrowing Body Shape on Atlas
